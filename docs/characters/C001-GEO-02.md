# LIVYNTH C001-GEO-02 — Morph-safe geometric fitting candidate

**Status: reproducible candidate constructed and locally audited, but NOT yet a recognizable Character 001.**

## What was implemented

- `geo/morph_fit.py`: small Gaussian radial-basis deformation of existing V32 face geometry using front-reference facial ratios, not a photograph-to-mesh reconstruction engine.
- `geo/character001_landmarks.json`: manual estimates of eyes, mouth corners and chin from the approved `01_subject_front.png` (1086 × 1448). These are approximate, not automatically detected or camera calibrated.
- `geo/audit_morph_fit.py`: checks exported GLB structure, skin weights, morph shape geometry, animation definitions and importability.
- `geo/tests/test_morph_fit.py`: tests binary GLB editing, control field/Jacobian, morph transport and safety checks.

The source avatar is the existing **`our-avatar-v32-clean-blink.glb`** (40,463,720 bytes), *not* the separate `ours-photoreal.glb` (different topology). Use the V32 file specifically. The source has seven skin definitions, one animation, and 122 morph targets across five meshes (23, 13, 8, 12, 66).

## Technical method and limits

The existing V32 morph-rich facial mesh and eyeball coordinates supply approximate 3D source anchors. 2D front-reference image ratios map to the source eye separation to estimate a chin and mouth-corner correction. Additional nose/brow/jaw adjustments are explicitly conservative heuristic hints, not observed 3D measurements. The fitting uses a smooth Gaussian control field; for each morph target, the deformed vertex delta equals `warp(rest + original_delta) - warp(rest)`. Morph normals are transported by the deformation Jacobian inverse transpose, avoiding inconsistent geometry and normals. Its effect on combinations of multiple simultaneously active shapes is approximate, not exact.

The algorithm intentionally checks for GLB 2 JSON+BIN format, valid accessors and bounds, a V32-like morph-rich source mesh, a detectable eyeball mesh, acceptable point positions, and positive deformation Jacobians. It does not solve the short hairstyle, head-pose camera calibration, accurate albedo/normal/roughness maps, or lip-sync timing.
